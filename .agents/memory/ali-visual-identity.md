---
name: ALi visual identity
description: Durable product direction for the visual hierarchy and tone of the ALi Inventaire interface.
---

Use a warm editorial naturalist identity throughout ALi Inventaire: ivory canvas, high-contrast forest-green ink, lively ochre field accents, serif display headings, readable sans-serif body text, and restrained framing. The global shell foregrounds the ALi brand, current project, and continuous conversation. The map is important supporting context, not the dominant first impression.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this direction for the entire application after providing a visual reference, replacing the earlier monochrome black interface.

**How to apply:** Extend the same visual grammar to every new screen, dialogue, result, table, loading state, empty state, and mobile view. Body copy must remain comfortably readable on Android, controls need touch-friendly sizing, and nested evidence blocks should not accumulate redundant borders, shadows, and padding. Preserve the genuine scripted dialogue and scientific evidence hierarchy rather than turning the experience into a generic dashboard.
