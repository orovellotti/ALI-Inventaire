---
name: ALi visual identity
description: Durable product direction for the visual hierarchy and tone of the ALi Inventaire interface.
---

Use a light, editorial naturalist identity throughout ALi Inventaire: ivory canvas, deep forest green, restrained botanical accents, serif display headings, calm sans-serif reading text, fine borders, and generous spacing. The global shell foregrounds the ALi brand, current project, and continuous conversation. The map is important supporting context, not the dominant first impression.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this direction for the entire application after providing a visual reference, replacing the earlier monochrome black interface.

**How to apply:** Extend the same visual grammar to every new screen, dialogue, result, table, loading state, empty state, and mobile view. Preserve the genuine scripted dialogue and scientific evidence hierarchy rather than turning the experience into a generic dashboard.
