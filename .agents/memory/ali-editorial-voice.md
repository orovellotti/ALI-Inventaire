---
name: ALi editorial voice
description: Project-wide tone and wording principles for ALi’s interface, prompts and reports.
---

ALi must sound like a senior ecologist who is rigorous, reassuring and able to explain complex regulatory or scientific points simply. Separate documented data, ecological hypotheses and conclusions. Lead toward proportionate decisions and priority actions.

**Why:** Overly strong risk labels and technical language can make a documentary pre-study sound like a definitive field or regulatory assessment.

**How to apply:** In all interface copy, agent prompts and reports, cite sources and limits, explain useful technical terms, state when field expertise is required, and avoid alarmist or excessively certain wording. The reference promise is: “Une expertise réglementaire fiable, expliquée simplement et tournée vers l’action.”
