name: International scope
description: English default, worldwide coverage boundaries and client referral for ALi studies.
---

ALi targets worldwide study locations with English as the default interface and report language. Country-specific evidence must remain geographically scoped; worldwide availability is not a claim of complete local regulatory or biodiversity coverage.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose worldwide analyses with country-adapted sources and limits, rather than an English interface restricted to France.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinctions between documented records, potential presence and field-confirmed presence. Never apply French protections or regional assessments to another country, or interpret unavailable local sources as no ecological issues. Final conclusions should suggest engaging Natural Solutions experts via https://natural-solutions.eu for field investigations and expert validation, without inventing service availability or regulatory guarantees.

For a study spanning multiple countries, prefer explicitly unavailable territorial conclusions to merging the strongest country-specific status into one study-wide classification.

**Why:** Merging a French and foreign observation of the same taxon can otherwise carry a French protection flag or scoring boost into the foreign assessment. The conservative study-wide approach deliberately trades some territorial detail for avoiding false jurisdictional claims.

**How to apply:** If finer country-by-country reporting is added later, preserve per-observation jurisdiction and evidence provenance before reintroducing local classifications.
